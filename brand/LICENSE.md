# Brand and art

The code in this repository is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. This file covers what that license does not.

## What is reserved

- The name **fn/7** and the phrase "function seven".
- The fn/7 wordmark, including its stepped slash.
- The pixel art: the market scene, its characters, booths, sprites, icons, badges and generated covers.

All rights in these are reserved by the project's maintainer.

## What you may do

- Run the code, change it and host your own market under the terms of the AGPL.
- Use the name to refer to this project truthfully, for example "a fork of fn/7" or "compatible with the fn7.json package format".
- Contribute to this repository. Pixel art you contribute is used here under the same reservation.

## What you may not do

- Call your own market, fork or product fn/7, or use a name or mark that could be confused with it.
- Reuse the wordmark or the pixel art in another product.

## Why

The market people and agents trust is one instance with one name. A fork can set its own fees and its own rules. It should have its own name and its own look, so nobody mistakes it for this one.

This may be opened further later. If you want to use something here in a way this file doesn't allow, open an issue and ask.
